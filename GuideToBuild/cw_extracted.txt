Description:
You are a database developer working for a transportation service provider called SmartMove Transport Solutions. The company provides passenger transportation services through buses, vans, and other vehicles for daily commuters, corporate employees, and special travel requirements.
The system manages vehicle operations, driver assignments, passenger registrations, route planning, trip scheduling, ticket reservations, payment processing, and customer feedback. The platform aims to improve transportation efficiency by providing real-time trip management, digital booking facilities, and centralized transport operations.
The system offers a wide range of functionalities, including vehicle management, route management, driver management, passenger management, online ticket booking, payment processing, and trip monitoring.
Design the ER or EER diagram.
Develop a simple Web or Enterprise Application for the  SmartMove Transport Solutions system using any suitable programming language or framework.
Design and implement a relational database schema using Oracle Database.
The database must support core system operations, including:
Vehicle Management 
Driver Management 
Route Management 
Passenger Management 
Trip Scheduling Management 
Ticket Booking Management 
Payment Processing  
Maintenance Management 
Feedback and Review Management 
Populate the database with sufficient sample data to simulate a realistic application scenario.
Write and implement PL/SQL programs (procedures, functions, cursors, and/or triggers with exception handling) to perform database operations and business logic.
Your PL/SQL implementation should include at least five(05) business reports.
Examples
Identifying the most frequently used routes based on ticket bookings.
Calculating total revenue generated from ticket sales within a given period.
Generating passenger travel history reports.
Finding vehicles due for maintenance. 
  MongoDB Integration
Implement MongoDB as a complementary NoSQL database to manage unstructured and dynamic transportation-related information. 
MongoDB collections should capture:
Vehicle images and documents. 
Passenger reviews and feedback comments.
Travel announcements and notifications.
Trip-related multimedia content. 
Ensure data is stored in a schema-flexible format to support dynamic content and community interactions.
Demonstrate queries in MongoDB to,
Retrieve all passenger reviews for a specific route.
Identify highest-rated vehicles or drivers based on customer feedback.
Search customer complaints or feedback using specific keywords.
Retrieve vehicle documents and multimedia information. 
Integrate MongoDB with the application layer to provide real-time content delivery and interactive forums for students and instructors.
